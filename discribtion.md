## The issue 
Hilton Hotel is unhappy with its booking system:
* They get more reservations than they have space for.
* Guests cancel their reservation, leaving empty rooms. Some even don't cancel and don't show up at all.
* If they could have a system to decide when to accept a reservation, they could increase their revenue.
* They can overbook, but in case they run out of room, they will need to pay for a room in another hotel at, in average, 2.5x the rate.

## Judging Criteria
* Performance: net revenue calculation
* Explainability: in your presentation you should explain to a non-technical stakeholder how your model works and how it reaches its redommendations.
